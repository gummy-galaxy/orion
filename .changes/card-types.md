Cards now carry types, which say what a card is and how it counts toward blocking and progress. `orion card types` and `orion card type-show` describe them, `orion card attach-type` and `orion card detach-type` add and take off types on a card, and `--where 'type = "gate"'` finds cards by any type they carry.
