# Engineering

Engineering pages cover how Orion is built: its architecture, its public interfaces, and proposals for changing them.

An audit or proposal is a snapshot. It recommends changes as of the day it was written, and once a change is settled, the call moves into [`DECISIONS.md`](https://github.com/vocksel/orion/blob/main/DECISIONS.md). For what Orion accepts today, see the [CLI](/reference/cli/) and [MCP](/reference/mcp) reference.
