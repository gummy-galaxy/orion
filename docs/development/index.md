# Development

These pages are about how Orion is made rather than how to use it. They're for anyone working on Orion, or curious about why it works the way it does.

- [Design](/development/design/) covers how Orion looks and feels: visual direction, interaction, and studies of ideas that may or may not ship.
- [Engineering](/development/engineering/) covers how Orion is built: architecture, public interfaces, and proposals for changing them.

Two pages sit above both. The [charter](/development/charter) sets Orion's direction: its principles, what it won't do, and the roadmap. The [decisions](/development/decisions) record the specific calls made along the way and why. A page here can recommend a change, and once the change is settled, the call moves into the decisions.
