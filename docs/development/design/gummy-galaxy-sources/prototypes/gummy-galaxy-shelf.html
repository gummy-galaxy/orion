Cards are drawn at 264 × 380 and shown here at 80%. Each group gets one of five art palettes, and the dot screen sits over all of them. Top left holds priority and size, top right the one status that asks for action, the center tile the kind, and the bottom row open blockers, checks in motion, and what finishing unblocks.
