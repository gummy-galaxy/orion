Gummy Galaxy

Concept art for Gummy Galaxy as a game, and for Orion as its first world. Seven worlds in one orbit, the emblem each one leaves behind, and the little star who travels between them. Rendered in gummy, clay, felt, gouache, riso, and pixels.

pieces sets
Rendered locally in Blender's Cycles, painted as hand-written SVG, separated into riso inks and pixel art with Pillow, and finished with grain. Kitbash pieces use CC0 models and HDRIs from Poly Haven and KayKit by Kay Lousberg as a base.
