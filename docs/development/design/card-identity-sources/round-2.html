Skin · constellations

Fourteen skies

The figures are now placed by hand instead of sampled from the icons, and each card gets its own sky. J12 to J14 borrow from Skyrim's skill constellations: smoky skies, flared stars, the sign drawn faintly behind, and stars that light up as the work moves forward.
