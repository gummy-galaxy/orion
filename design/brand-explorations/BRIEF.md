# Orion brand exploration brief

## Product feeling

Orion is a map of work that should also feel rewarding to touch and explore. The visual language can borrow from constellations, paths, stickers, and the motion of objects on the canvas. Aim for a soft, vivid, gummy feel without losing the clarity needed to read a busy work graph.

## Naming directions

- **Orion:** Keep the existing name and make the three Belt stars a recognizable signature. Connect them to a larger constellation so the mark also suggests cards and dependencies.
- **Star Charter:** Treat work as navigation. Explore chart folds, waypoints, routes, compass forms, and the act of mapping a course.
- **Artemis:** Explore a forest green hunter identity with a bow, arrow, moon, and straight route toward a goal. It should feel decisive and dependable.

## Icon system

- Begin with a square app icon that reads at 64 px in the Dock and at 256 px in a product view.
- Carry the chosen motif into the favicon and toolbar with simple silhouettes, not tiny decorative detail.
- Give different node kinds and states a distinct shape or pattern as well as color. Text labels remain visible.
- Keep enough contrast in light and dark themes, and respect reduced motion when adding playful effects.

## Current studies

The six SVGs in this folder cover two icon directions for each name. The `previews/` folder holds 256 px and 64 px renders and three comparison images. These are concepts for choosing a direction; the app icon has not been changed.

## What to decide next

Choose a name and one motif to refine. Judge each at Dock size, against a crowded desktop, and beside real canvas cards. Then develop the wordmark, favicon, toolbar icons, and node shape vocabulary as one family.
